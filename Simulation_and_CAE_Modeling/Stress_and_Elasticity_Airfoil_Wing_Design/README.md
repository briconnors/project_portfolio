## Stress & Elasticity:   Airfoil Wing Design
*Created a quadcopter wing simulation using Excel to map the aerodynamic loads across varying angles of attack, with detailed stress analysis of two cases in ANSYS.*

My group explored the structural feasibility of the addition of airfoil motor mounts to a drone designed to lift sandbags to help combat forest fires. I solved for the potential force inputs dependent on the attack angle to find optimal testing points. The design utilized a rotated reference frame so that it could be easily reused with only the airfoil angle needing to be changed. In simulations, a quarter model of the wing was used due to symmetry and to focus on critical stress locations on the wing. 

The primary concentration was at the bottom of the connection to the hub, since motor thrust acts at the end of a fuselage. In the section view along the cross section of the foil, it's evident that the stress remains primarily along the surface. However, the tip of the trailing edge connection exhibits uniform high stress, indicating a potential fracture point. 

My partner evaluated the problem according to traditional beam bending behavior using the moment of inertia of the cross sectional area, which supported the stress trends observed in the simulation.
