## Engineering Design & Problem-Solving:   Sun Tracking Solar Panel
*Worked with a multidisciplinary team to design a sun tracking solar panel, mimicking the biology of sunflowers (heliotropism) by rotating at the base to maximize perpendicular contact with its rays throughout the day.*

I conceptualized the design, 3D printed custom connector parts and LDR cones, fashioned the PVC pipe support, lasercut the electrical housing from acrylic, and assembled the stepper motor mechanism, which was triggered by photoresistors through Arduino. My group members developed the controller and sensor logic, while I integrated the mechanical design to ensure compatibility with actuation requirements.

The system was tested in a dark room with a light moved to various locations, and regardless of distance the panel consistently tracked the source. 

Although the initial concept explored two degrees of freedom, time constraints led us to implement base rotation with a manually adjustable tilt angle to accommodate seasonal changes.
